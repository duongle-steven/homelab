    Step 9: Logs with Loki 3 and Grafana Alloy, in production and the sandbox

    Status: design approved in chat 2026-10-17, written spec awaiting review. Branch: feat/logs (PR into dev, after step 8b's PR #35 merges).

    Goal

    Every pod's logs and the cluster's Kubernetes events land in Loki and stay queryable in Grafana for 30 days, in production and in the sandbox, where tests prove the path. The deprecated loki-stack chart (Loki 2.x, Promtail) is replaced by the maintained loki chart (single binary) and Grafana Alloy. The roadmap gains this step as 9; Git and CI, Backups, Nodes and Remote access become 10 to 13.

    Context

    • system/loki wraps loki-stack 2.10.1 (Grafana's repository): Loki 2.9 with Promtail, ServiceMonitor on. It runs only in production (the root ApplicationSet takes system/*; the sandbox's list omits it and system/monitoring-system/values-dev.yaml drops Grafana's Loki data source). Its allow admits Grafana and Prometheus on 3100; scripts/check-mesh-policies has Grafana → Loki in EXTRA.
    • Upstream master and nixos use the same chart: Promtail as a DaemonSet reading /var/log/pods (and /var/lib/docker/containers) through hostPath; neither collects Kubernetes events.
    • Found 2026-10-17: Grafana's repository marks loki-stack deprecated (last 2.10.3, Loki 2.9.3); loki-distributed and lgtm-distributed are deprecated too; no maintained all-in-one chart exists. Promtail reached end of life on 2026-03-02; Grafana Alloy replaces it. The loki chart moved to https://grafana-community.github.io/helm-charts (Grafana's own copy stopped at 7.3.0); simple scalable mode is deprecated before Loki 4.0. k8s-monitoring (Alloy with ready-made pipelines) overlaps kube-prometheus-stack.
    • k3s runs containerd: the kubelet writes every container's log under /var/log/pods; /var/lib/docker/containers is empty there.
    • loki.source.kubernetes_events supports Alloy's clustering: watching every namespace, only one clustered instance collects; positions live in Alloy's storage path.
    • Decided 2026-10-17: separate charts (loki, plain alloy), Alloy as a DaemonSet reading files like upstream's Promtail, events kept 30 days, the step numbered 9.
    • Versions (2026-10-17): chart loki 18.17.0 (Loki 3.7.8), chart alloy 1.13.1 (Alloy v1.20.1).

    Decisions

    Topic Decision
    Component system/loki (namespace loki) wraps both charts, as upstream wraps loki-stack
    Loki deploymentMode: SingleBinary, 1 replica, filesystem storage on a 10Gi PVC, schema tsdb v13, auth_enabled: false, retention 30 days (compactor)
    Loki extras off gateway, canary, chunks and results caches, minio, helm test, self-monitoring dashboards that need the operator
    Collector Alloy DaemonSet; discovery.kubernetes (pods on its node) → loki.source.file on /var/log/pods (hostPath, read-only) → loki.process (stage.cri) → loki.write to http://loki:3100
    Labels namespace, pod, container, app (the pod's app.kubernetes.io/name, else app), node; stream from the CRI line
    Positions Alloy's storage path on a hostPath (/var/lib/alloy), so a restarted pod resumes where it stopped
    Events loki.source.kubernetes_events, all namespaces, clustering on (one instance collects), labels namespace, job
    RBAC Alloy's chart ClusterRole: pods and nodes (discovery), events (get, list, watch)
    Mesh Alloy stays in the ambient mesh; same-namespace calls are allowed; Prometheus scrapes Alloy (12345) and Loki (3100)
    Grafana Data source http://loki.loki:3100 in production and the sandbox
    Sandbox system/loki joins the sandbox's root list; smaller PVC and retention (values-dev.yaml)
    Roadmap Step 9 Logs; old 9 to 12 become 10 to 13 everywhere they are named
    Backup Not in this step: step 11 decides whether Loki's PVC is backed up (default: not)

    Design

    system/loki

    Chart.yaml depends on loki 18.17.0 (grafana-community) and alloy 1.13.1 (grafana). values.yaml:

    • loki: single binary as above; loki.limits_config.retention_period: 720h, compactor with retention enabled and its delete-request store on the filesystem; singleBinary.persistence.size: 10Gi; ServiceMonitor on (the chart's monitoring block), everything that needs Grafana Agent operator or Loki's own canary off.
    • alloy: controller.type: daemonset; alloy.clustering.enabled: true; mounts /var/log/pods read-only and /var/lib/alloy (hostPath, DirectoryOrCreate) as storage; runs as root (the log files are root's, read-only mount); config in alloy.configMap.content (River) with the pipeline above and the events source; ServiceMonitor on.
    • templates/authorization-policy.yaml: unchanged rules (same namespace, Grafana and Prometheus on 3100) plus Prometheus on Alloy's 12345.

    values-dev.yaml: PVC 2Gi, retention 7 days.

    Wiring

    • system/argocd/values-dev.yaml: system/loki in the root directories.
    • system/monitoring-system/values-dev.yaml: drop the override that removes Grafana's Loki data source.
    • scripts/check-mesh-policies: Grafana → Loki stays; the ServiceMonitor checks see the new ports.
    • Egress: nothing new (the API server is in the baseline).

    Roadmap renumbering

    CLAUDE.md's roadmap inserts "9. Logs: Loki 3 (single binary) and Grafana Alloy (pod logs and Kubernetes events), in the sandbox too (done)" and renumbers 9–12 to 10–13; every "step 9/10/11/12" in docs, comments, CLAUDE.md and docs/superpowers/deferred-minors.md follows (about 15 places, plus those 8b added).

    Error handling

    • Loki down or its PVC full: Alloy keeps the lines in its write-ahead log (loki.write's wal, experimental in Alloy v1.20, on the host's /var/lib/alloy, segments kept 24 hours) and sends them once Loki is back; older segments are dropped; kube-prometheus-stack's pod alerts (crash loops, PVC filling up) reach ntfy.
    • A pod deleted before Alloy read its last lines: they are lost (the kubelet removes its log directory).
    • The events instance moves to another Alloy pod (node drain): a few events may be delivered twice.
    • Losing Loki's PVC loses past logs only. Whether step 11 (backups) declares Loki's PVC is decided there; until then logs have no backup.

    Testing

    • Offline: renders (prod, dev), scripts/check-mesh-policies, scripts/check-env-config, scripts/check-secret-stores.
    • Sandbox (test/logs_test.go, through the API server's service proxy as prometheusQuery does):
    • a temporary pod prints a marker; a LogQL query {namespace="<ns>", pod="<pod>"} |= "<marker>" returns it within a few minutes, with the labels above;
    • a Kubernetes event of that namespace (for example the pod's Scheduled or Pulled) is in Loki;
    • after the Alloy pod on the node restarts, a second marker arrives and the first is not delivered again;
    • Grafana's Loki data source health check succeeds (Grafana API, admin user);
    • Prometheus has Loki's and Alloy's targets up.
    • make dev-clean; make dev: every test passes; the make dev filter gains Logs.
